# Design spec — Vishal Nair → TMF Group WIN deck (6 slides)

## Brand identity
Brand research: tmf-group.com and Wikimedia are blocked by this environment's network policy; web search returned no official palette.
Palette taken from the client's draft (built on TMF public branding):
- Navy 142D43 — dominant (dark slides, titles, panels) ~60%
- Teal 087E83 — secondary (kickers, numbered markers, labels)
- Crimson CE1734 — sharp accent, used sparingly (key stat numbers on light bg, small markers)
- Ice F0F5F6 — light panels; Mist D5DFE3 — hairlines; Slate 526575 — muted text; Aqua A9D7DB — labels on navy
- White FFFFFF content backgrounds
Fonts: Arial (headings, bold) + Calibri (body). Draft used Aptos → replaced with safe fonts.
Sizes: title 28–40pt bold; kicker 12pt caps teal (charSpacing); subtitle 18pt slate; body 14pt; labels 12–13pt bold; captions/sources/footer 11pt.

## Design system
16:9 LAYOUT_WIDE 13.33×7.5in; 0.5in margins; gaps 0.3in. Content slides: kicker y0.45, title y0.7 (one line), subtitle y1.35, content 2.05–6.55, sources y6.65, footer y7.0 (VISHAL NAIR / TMF GROUP / WIN • CANDIDATE PROPOSAL + page no.).
Motif: numbered teal circles + navy "proof" panels holding CAR track-record stats. No accent stripes/underlines. Sandwich: dark title, light content, dark proof panel on close.
Logo: none available (draft contained a recreated text wordmark, not an official asset → not reused). Empty reserved logo zone top-left of slide 1 (0.6,0.5, 2.2×0.8in) and top-right of slide 6 panel. Flag "Logo missing".

## Slide-by-slide
1. Title (dark navy). Left 60%: kicker, 40pt white title, 3 outlined pills for pillars, name/role and contact at bottom. Right: abstract concentric-circle network motif (native shapes, teal/aqua, transparent).
2. Company & alignment (white). Service lines as subtitle. Three rows: TMF statement (navy label + text) → arrow → "My alignment" in ice panel. Bottom: 4 numbered navy roadmap chips (01–04).
3. Priority 1 (layout A): left ice panel "The challenge" (Signals / Impact / JD calls for); right "The solution" with 3 numbered steps, evidence line, navy CAR panel with 18% crimson-on-white stat.
4. Priority 2 (layout B): challenge as 3 horizontal ice cards across top; below: solution steps left, evidence + navy CAR panel (22%) right.
5. Priority 3 (layout A mirrored): solution left, challenge ice panel right, CAR panel with 30% and 35%.
6. Roadmap & why me: left vertical 4-phase timeline (teal nodes) + day-180 targets box; right navy panel "Why hire me" with 2×2 stat grid (18/22/35/30%) and credentials; commitment line bottom-left.
